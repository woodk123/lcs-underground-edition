Original design prompt
======================

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production 2D isometric building sprite atlas for a contemporary American city strategy game, NOT a concept illustration or interface.
Primary request: Create a 1024 by 1024 square transparent PNG atlas consisting of EXACTLY 16 independent isolated building sprites arranged in a STRICT evenly spaced 4 columns by 4 rows grid. Each cell is exactly one quarter of the image width and height. Do not draw grid lines. Each building must stay wholly within its own cell with generous transparent gutter, no overlap and no cropping.
Camera/style: Identical orthographic isometric camera in all 16 cells, looking southeast, 30 degree isometric ground axes, crisp ink contours, hand-painted graphic novel architectural detail, clear silhouette readable at 100 pixels. Dusk palette of deep navy ink, warm stone/paper, weathered brick, muted teal roofs, restrained vermilion accents, small amber-lit windows. Same lighting from upper left. Architectural game sprites, NOT tiny pixel art. No surrounding scene.
Layout contract: Row-major order, top left to bottom right: row 1 = modest 3-storey brick apartment safehouse, single-storey neighborhood shop, small welcoming cafe with a red awning, brick loading warehouse. Row 2 = industrial factory with a smokestack, medium newspaper/media office, classical courthouse with columns, municipal police station. Row 3 = modern hospital, university academic hall, neoclassical bank, prison building with small enclosed yard. Row 4 = modern office tower, detached suburban house, farmhouse and small barn combined as one sprite, civic city hall with clock tower. Each structure is a distinct recognizable typology, approximately 80 percent of the cell's width, scale taller buildings down to fit.
Anchor: Within EACH cell center the footprint horizontally. Every building's lowest solid foot/ground contact reaches the same local y=224 of a 256 pixel cell, leaving 32 pixels below. Fit all roofs, chimneys, fences within local x=20..236 and y=12..224. Foundation planes are small and match the individual building, no road slabs. A narrow soft contact shadow may lie only inside the sprite footprint. Sprite sheet itself fully transparent between buildings.
Constraints: no lettering, signage text, numerals, labels, names, UI, decorative frames, people, vehicles, continuous streets, large terrain tiles, background, watermark or checkerboard drawn into pixels. True alpha transparency is essential. All 16 buildings fully separate and centered in their exact cells.


Selected revision
=================

Preserve the 16 recognizable architectural designs, row-major type order, ink graphic novel texture and palette, isometric camera and alpha transparency. Correct ONLY sprite sheet geometry and spacing so a game can crop frames in an exact 4 by 4 grid without touching neighboring sprites.
Output a square PNG canvas. IMPORTANT do NOT tightly crop or trim the canvas to the content; keep a wide transparent outer border. Divide the square mathematically into 16 equal cells (4 columns, 4 rows), with cell centers at x and y12.5%,37.5%,62.5%,87.5% of the canvas. No visible grid lines.
Resize and recenter each individual building, retaining the design, to fit entirely INSIDE its own equal cell: at least 12% of a CELL WIDTH transparent on LEFT and RIGHT, and at least 12% CELL HEIGHT transparent at TOP and BOTTOM. No pixels, smoke, shadow, fence, trees or roofs outside these safe bounds. Entire building sprite including optional shadow fits local x=12%..88% and y=12%..88%. All sprites share a common base/foot anchor at local x=50%, y=86%. Buildings must remain completely separated by transparent gutters. Scale the factory smoke down to stay within factory's own cell, do not let smoke drift into the apartment above.
Retain exactly16 sprites,4x4; no extra scene, ground under whole sheet, labels, UI, borders or text. Genuine transparent pixels around and between every sprite. The objective is technically clean fixed-grid game artwork, not a filled contact sheet. Preserve the full square canvas; do not crop away the transparent margins.
