Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: Original UX concept for a modern desktop strategy game, landscape 16:9, polished single screen with an inset detail.
Primary request: A modern, playable-looking management interface for Liberal Crime Squad: New Age, inspired by the spatial city management and crew dossiers of classic organized crime strategy games. Show exactly how a player clicks a person or building in the central workspace, then assigns work to that object. This is a new 2023 Seattle political satire strategy game, not a 1920s mafia game.
Scene/backdrop: One high fidelity app screen. Narrow dark ink sidebar at left; small calm date and money strip at top. Most of the middle is a beautiful original isometric city district illustration showing contemporary brick apartment blocks, corner stores, public square, a small warehouse and a safehouse. An outlined safehouse block is selected, with a small clear contextual callout attached to that block. Bottom third of the central workspace contains three generously sized crew dossier cards with a diverse adult activist crew, portraits, names, a readable health indicator, a location and a current assignment. One expanded selected card offers large direct action buttons 'Assign activity', 'Equipment', 'View dossier'. Do not use a detached right-hand menu.
Style/medium: Modern strategy game UI, editorial illustrated isometric city with restrained realistic geometry and soft atmospheric shadows. Warm paper panels, dark blue black ink, muted desaturated teal, warm sandstone architecture, selective vermilion highlight. Matte painted artwork, readable contemporary sans-serif typography, no fake DOS text or terminal. Light subtle dossier/case-file details, excellent clear whitespace and disciplined grids. Intuitive object-first interactions. High contrast, visually inviting, believable production interface.
Composition/framing: Full screen UI at 1920x1080 proportions. City artwork 60 percent of central screen; three readable crew cards in bottom central band. Narrow sidebar approximately 160px. Small compact inline safehouse contextual action panel attached to selected location, no right column. A single tiny callout 'Select a person or place to give an order' unobtrusive at top of city.
Text (verbatim): App title 'LIBERAL CRIME SQUAD' subtitle 'NEW AGE'. Left navigation 'Headquarters', 'Crew', 'Assignments', 'City', 'Agenda', 'Reports'. Central header 'Seattle' and 'Plan your next move'. Top date 'January 1, 2023' money '$7'. Selected location label 'Safehouse'. Contextual actions 'Manage base', 'Send squad'. Crew section 'Your crew'. Three people 'Bernie Spitzer', 'Alex Morgan', 'Jamie Chen'. Selected Bernie role 'Organizer', location 'Safehouse', activity 'Causing trouble'. Selected card actions 'Assign activity', 'Equipment', 'View dossier'. Bottom primary action 'Review & advance day'.
Constraints: Entirely original artwork and fictional faces. Show information hierarchy, central direct click affordances, and city ownership/location identity. UI labels clear and correctly spelled, avoid too many labels. Artwork is an idea for a future city view; no suggestion that simulated streets or territories are implemented. Avoid prohibited game logos, copied Gangsters screenshots, guns, fedora stereotype, neon cyberpunk, tiny monospaced tables, letter hotkey menus, duplicate Available choices sidebar, visual clutter, paper cursive text, photoreal browser chrome.
